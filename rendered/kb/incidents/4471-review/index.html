Two causes, two fixes

Parser: the tenant writes day first; the shared parser reads month first

For day-first tenants, an ambiguous date such as 07/04/2026 must mean 7 April, not 4 July. The shared parser uses month-first interpretation. This path includes app and email reports, and form reports where the hint is not the separate booking-v3 conflict.

Reproduce with Harbour Dental (en-GB, booking-v2) through the app. Enter 07/04/2026 and compare the intended date with the invite date. Report R-41200 describes this swap. The export provides the reported symptom and the desk’s classification logic, not runnable product code; this reproduction must be verified against the product before claiming a fix.

Fix the shared parser’s locale handling. Test ambiguous dates, unambiguous day-first dates, and month-first tenants as controls. Do not assume this also fixes the form hint.

Hint: booking-v3 tells day-first tenants to enter a month-first value

Booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY, but its form reads input in the tenant’s day-first format. For Alder Physio (de-DE, booking-v3), follow the hint and enter 08/06/2026 for 6 August: the form reads 8 June. R-41241 reports the resulting 8 June / 6 August mismatch.

Align the displayed hint and the form’s interpretation for each tenant. Verify Alder Physio, Copper Studio, and Pale Harbour. Also test app/email paths for those tenants: their reports belong to the shared-parser issue, not the form-hint issue. This proposed reproduction also needs product verification; the export does not include the booking application.
